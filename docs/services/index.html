Monthly Content

Short-form clips every month, batched into shoot days so it never eats your week.

Frequently asked questions

Is my first video really free?

Yes. One short vertical video (up to 60 seconds) for a new client, filmed at your business in North Jersey and edited with music and captions, ready to post. One per business. No contract and no catch. It is the easiest way to see how we work.

How long have you been doing this?

Since 2022. Reliable Kev Media has spent the last few years filming towns, local businesses and trade companies across North Jersey.

How long until I get my video?

Most projects are delivered in 2–3 weeks, depending on the scope of work. Bigger projects get a timeline agreed before we start.

How much does it cost?

A single short video is $150, filmed and edited, ready to post. A half day (up to 4 hours) starts at $500 and includes your choice of one long-form YouTube video or three short clips. A full day story (up to 8 hours) starts at $1,100 and includes a planning call, a sit-down Q&A with you, one long-form video and three short clips. Drone footage can be added from $300. Every project gets a clear quote after a free planning call, before anything is filmed. Brand films, the podcast and monthly content are quoted by the project.

What's the difference between a package and a custom quote?

A package (a single video, a half day or a full day story) is for when you want us to come film: an event, a property, or your business and a few questions with you. A custom quote is for full storytelling projects like brand films, the podcast and monthly content, which take more planning, directing and editing.

Do I get the raw footage?

Your price includes the edited videos listed. Raw footage is not included, but it can be added to any project. Just ask and we will include it in your quote.

Can I get my video faster?

Every project gets the same care, so edited videos are delivered in 2–3 weeks. Rush delivery is not offered.

Can I add more videos or hours?

Yes. Anything beyond what is included, like extra videos, extra hours or extra revisions, is quoted before any work begins, so you always know the price up front.

What gear do you use?

Two Canon 90D cameras with a range of lenses, tripods and lighting, plus a DJI Flip drone for aerial shots. Two cameras means interviews and podcasts are covered from two angles.

Where do you film?

All over North Jersey: Bergen, Passaic, Essex, Morris and Hudson County. If you are a little outside that, ask.

Do I need to be comfortable on camera?

No. We direct the shoot and keep it relaxed, so you can just talk about what you know. No script and no camera experience needed.

What happens after I reach out?

We start with a free planning call. We learn about your business and what you want people to know, then send you a plan and a price.

Can I combine services?

Yes, and most clients do. A podcast episode can feed a month of clips, and event coverage can turn into a brand film. Drone footage can be added to any of them.
